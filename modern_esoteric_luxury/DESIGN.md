## Brand & Style

This design system expresses modern esoteric luxury: an elevated, mystical atmosphere anchored by algorithmic precision and deep human credibility. Designed for individuals seeking genuine Vedic and modern astrological clarity without cartoonish tropes, the experience evokes introspection, quiet reverence, and unshakeable trust.

The aesthetic fuses deep nocturnal minimalism with subtle celestial glassmorphism:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Layered midnight fields punctuated by soft nebular glows, avoiding flat pitch black in favor of resonant indigo-tinged voids.
- **Astral Metallics:** Restrained, hairline gold accents reminiscent of vintage brass astrolabes and celestial cartography, applied with clinical restraint rather than opulence.
- **Glassmorphic Translucency:** Frosted, low-sheen surfaces simulating smoked obsidian and lens optics, maintaining contrast and structural legibility.
- **Authoritative Editorial Presence:** The cadence of an intellectual manuscript combined with the responsiveness of a premier AI interface.

## Colors

The palette establishes an nocturnal realm illuminated by celestial lights and metallurgical precision:

- **Primary (`#D4AF37` / Imperial Astral Gold):** Reserved for moments of definitive truth, celestial alignments, verified certification seals, primary actionable triggers, and hairline highlight rims. Never overused as solid block backgrounds for large interactive areas; it performs as refined ornamentation and focal emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#8B5CF6` / Celestial Violet):** Communicates computational intelligence, cosmic currents, active states, and AI remedy generation progress.
- **Tertiary (`#3B2D54` / Deep Aether):** Forms subtle radial ambient vignettes, segmented control backplates, and inner container shading.
- **Neutral Core (`#0B0D1B` & `#0F1226` / Deep Midnight & Astral Slate):** The bedrock canvas. Surfaces layer upon this baseline through varying alpha transparencies and slate-tinted borders.
- **Starlight Highlights (`#F8FAFC`, `#E2E8F0`):** Text elements and planetary glyphs leverage starlight tiers to guarantee WCAG AAA legibility against profound darkness.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional dialogue between ancient astrological treatises and razor-sharp computational intelligence:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries classical cadence, literary dignity, and mystical intrigue without descending into theatrical antiqueness. Used for natal house titles, transit ephemeris headers, remedy prescriptions, and hero statements. Letter spacing remains neutral or slightly tightened for display scales.
- **Body & Numerical Readouts (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Ensures flawless clarity for technical ephemeris data, birth chart degrees, planetary aspects, and INR (`₹`) financial values. High x-height and open apertures prevent fatigue across analytical readings.
- **Labels & Micro-Tokens:** Set in uppercase medium/semibold weights with generous letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to mirror luxury horology and astronomical charts.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout relies on an 8pt base grid disciplined by calm, breathable negative space that mirrors cosmic expanse:

- **Canvas Margins:** Fixed outer margins on mobile (`1rem` / 16px) ensure maximum screen utilization for dense natal charts, transitioning to `2rem` (32px) on tablet and desktop canvases with a maximum reading container width of 1200px.
- **Column Grids:** 4 columns on mobile devices, 8 columns on tablet, and 12 columns on desktop. Gutters expand gracefully from 12px on handheld devices to 20px on wide viewports.
- **Component Breathing Room:** Components maintain generous interior padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`) to prevent dense metaphysical data (e.g., planetary transits, nakshatra calculations) from overwhelming the seeker.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use harsh drop shadows; instead, it is manifested through luminous atmospheric tiers and frosted obsidian strata:

- **Layer 0 (Base Canvas):** `#0B0D1B` infused with static radial gradients of violet mist (`rgba(59, 45, 84, 0.25)`) anchored behind focal interactive widgets.
- **Layer 1 (Frosted Glass Surfaces):** Background `rgba(15, 18, 38, 0.65)` layered with a `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an ultra-subtle border stroke of `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated Astrological Cards & Drawers):** Background `rgba(23, 27, 54, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Elevated cards receive a perimeter hairline gradient stroke: top-left transitioning from `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)` to bottom-right `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.1)`.
- **Glow & Starlight Shadows:** Elevated modal containers and active remedy cards cast a diffused astral aura: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.18), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The geometry balances the sacred geometry of the circle with refined modern UI architecture:

- **Curvature Hierarchy:** Standard surfaces, glass sheets, input fields, and consultation cards adopt an 8px (`0.5rem`) base radius, expanding to 16px (`1rem`) on larger structural modular frames.
- **Radial Celestial Formats:** Badges, zodiac wheel controls, planet nodes, and interactive pills utilize continuous capsule curves (`rounded-full` or pill-shaped geometries) to mirror orbits and astrolabes.
- **Border Fidelity:** Borders never exceed 1px hairline thickness. They emulate precise, metallic instruments rather than heavy borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Remedy Unlock / Kundli Generation):** Solid radial gradient from warm polished gold (`#F59E0B`) to deep imperial gold (`#D4AF37`), filled with dark midnight navy typography (`#0B0D1B`, bold). Crisp inner border highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary Action (Vedic Deep Dive):** Smoked glass background (`rgba(59, 45, 84, 0.4)`), hairline celestial violet border (`#8B5CF6`), starlight text (`#F8FAFC`). Hover triggers an astral glow (`0 0 16px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3)`).
- **Text Action:** Monoline starlight white with subtle underline or trailing astrological glyph (`→`, `✦`).

### Glowing Glassmorphism Cards
- Constructed with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, dark translucent core (`rgba(15, 18, 38, 0.6)`), and dual-tone metallic hairline borders. Top edge reveals an imperceptible starlight glimmer. Cards house horoscopic overviews, Dasha periods, and personalized AI remedy reports.

### 'Verified by Astrologer' Trust Badges
- Compact capsule badge (`rounded-full`). Deep indigo field (`rgba(59, 45, 84, 0.6)`) bounded by a hairline pure gold stroke (`#D4AF37`).
- Accompanied by a 12px solid gold star or check icon and micro typography (`label-sm`, uppercase), conferring authentic Vedic scholarship credentials alongside computational AI outputs.

### Zodiac Wheel Emblems
- Circular navigational and informational wheels featuring ultra-fine concentric coordinate rings (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.15)`). Active signs or transit planets are illuminated with gold halos (`#D4AF37`) and soft violet trailing paths.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Recessed slate background (`rgba(11, 13, 27, 0.8)`), 1px stroke of `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.12)`. When focused for birth date, time, or location inputs, the stroke illuminates with a dual astral violet halo (`#8B5CF6`) and transitions placeholder text from muted slate to crisp starlight white.

### Pricing Modules (INR ₹)
- Clean, uncluttered purchase tiers for detailed gemstone recommendations, Rudraksha sanctifications, and live pandit consults.
- Features prominent currency markers in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold, e.g., `₹1,499`, accompanied by subtle tax tags ("inc. Vedic consecration & shipping"). Primary tier highlighted by a hairline gold foil rim.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Segmented pills for switching chart systems (North Indian, South Indian, Western Tropical) resting on an obsidian rail with a smooth violet-tinted sliding pill. Checkbox indicators illuminate with gold astrological glyphs upon selection.
